# Outreach Templates

Short, specific, no adjectives the buyer cannot verify. Replace every bracket.
Never send any of these with a `[CONFIRM]` still in the text.

---

## 1. Cold email — social security board (Director / CEO)

**Subject:** Replacing the legacy administration system at `[Institution]`

> Dear `[Name]`,
>
> `[Institution]` runs contributions, compliance and benefits on
> `[legacy system, if known]`. Most boards in the region are facing the same
> two problems: the people who can maintain that system are retiring, and every
> policy change becomes a vendor project.
>
> We built and operate a complete social insurance administration platform —
> registration, contributions, compliance and enforcement, benefits from claim
> to payment, legal recovery, finance, and all citizen correspondence across
> email, SMS, WhatsApp, printed letters and an automated phone service. Benefit
> products, rates and letters are configured by your own staff under approval,
> not by us under invoice.
>
> Would a 30-minute working demonstration be useful? I would show real screens
> and real configuration, not slides.
>
> Regards,
> `[Name, title, organisation]`

## Rules for all outreach

1. Name the buyer's actual problem in the first two lines, or do not send it.
2. No superlatives, no "revolutionary", no "AI-powered" unless you are
   describing something specific that exists.
3. Never quote a figure that is not confirmed.
4. Ask for exactly one thing: a 30-minute demonstration.
5. If they say "send more information", send `10-one-pager.md` and
   `02-feature-catalogue.md` only — not the whole pack.
